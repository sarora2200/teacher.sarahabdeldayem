✨ Did you know? ✨

Letters change shape! Like a caterpillar turning into a butterfly 🦋. Most letters hold hands (connect) with the next letter, but some letters like ا د ذ ر ز و like to stand alone.

Look at the letter ب (bāʾ):
ب alone · بـ at start · ـبـ in middle · ـب at end.
