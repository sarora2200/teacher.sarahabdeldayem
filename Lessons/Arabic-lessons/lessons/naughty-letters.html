My Arabic Letters! 🎈

These 6 letters are super special! They hold hands with the letter before them… but NOT the letter after! 🤭

Meet the 6 Special Letters 👋

ا Alif “aa”
و Waw “w”
ر Ra “r”
ز Zay “z”
د Dal “d”
ذ Dhal “dh”
🫶 The Special Rule 🫶
These letters hold hands with the letter before them… but they let go of the letter after! ا و ر ز د ذ
